Our story

We built the tool we wished our own family shop had.

RetailInsight started as a weekend project to help one grocer stop guessing — it's now used by thousands of shop owners who'd rather sell than spreadsheet.

Mission

Make retail data usable by anyone, not just analysts.

Every small shop generates data. Almost none of them can read it. We close that gap in plain language, not dashboards full of jargon.

Vision

A world where "gut feeling" and "the numbers" finally agree.

We want every independent retailer — from a single stall to a five-branch chain — to make decisions with the same confidence as a data team twice their size.

Values
  • Clarity over complexity
  • Built with shop owners, not just for them
  • Data should be honest, even when it's uncomfortable
  • Small businesses deserve enterprise-grade tools
Our journey

From a spreadsheet to a platform.

Year 1

The first spreadsheet

A manual weekly sales tracker built for one shop, in one city — the entire idea started here.

Year 2

First 100 shops

Word of mouth among local retailers turned the spreadsheet into a proper login-based dashboard.

Year 3

Forecasting & alerts

Added sales forecasting and WhatsApp low-stock alerts — the two most-requested features.

Today

Thousands of shops, one mission

Still building around the same question we started with: what does this shop owner actually need to know today?
