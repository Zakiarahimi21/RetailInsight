The retail market we're building for.

A structured look at the environment small and mid-sized retailers operate in today — the same research that shaped RetailInsight's roadmap.

PESTLE Analysis

P

Political

Import duties and shifting VAT policy on retail goods affect small shops' margins directly.

E

Economic

Inflation and currency volatility push owners toward tighter inventory and cash-flow control.

S

Social

Customers increasingly expect digital receipts, loyalty perks, and fast, informal service.

T

Technological

Smartphone-first retailers are adopting mobile POS and cloud dashboards over desktop software.

L

Legal

Data protection expectations are rising even for small businesses handling customer records.

E

Environmental

Demand-based ordering (via forecasting) reduces overstock waste and spoilage.

SWOT Analysis

Strengths

  • Plain-language UI for non-technical owners
  • Fast setup, no IT team required
  • Alerts reach owners via WhatsApp/SMS

Weaknesses

  • Requires consistent data entry to stay accurate
  • Forecast quality depends on sales history depth

Opportunities

  • Large underserved market of unbanked-adjacent SMEs
  • Expansion into supplier/inventory marketplace features

Threats

  • Larger POS vendors adding lightweight analytics
  • Price sensitivity among smallest shop owners

Marketing Mix (4P)

Product

A retail intelligence dashboard: sales tracking, forecasting, and automated reporting in one login.

Price

Tiered monthly subscription (Starter / Professional / Business), priced below hiring part-time analytics help.

Place

Cloud-based, accessed via browser or mobile — no installation, works on shared shop devices.

Promotion

Word-of-mouth among retailer networks, local business associations, and free-trial-led signups.
